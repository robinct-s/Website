PRI.1

Phyto-Relay i.1 is a multisensory artefact object, reflecting on communion and non-human agency. The work reflects on humanity's perception of technology, divinity, and revelation: the impulse to locate transcendence in systems we cannot fully comprehend, and to pursue technological development as a form of progress, salvation, or gnosis. By framing the plant-machine relationship as a kind of non-agentic communion, phyto-relay i.1 considers how technological systems can take on ritual, prophetic, or sacred qualities, even as the human subject recedes from the scene. What remains is a material and unstable ecology of signals, growth, and response.

This system is an audio-visual feedback loop, wherein the plant interfaces with its ecology without will. It acts as performer, effector and effected, within a system in the wake of, and removed from, human control.

This work was commissioned by the School of Digital Art, MMU and was exhibited from 05.06.2026 to 18.06.2026.
