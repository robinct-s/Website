World Cup is a record label established in 2026.

The label launches with Feint, a 15 track various artists compilation curating a collage of takes on ambient, experimental trance music from around the world. Feint is a celebration of the invisible exchanges that shape contemporary electronic music. Rather than defining a scene, Feint hopes to celebrate the global threads that span and dissolve geography, culture and genre.

World Cup is created by Robin Tetlow-Shooter, Etienne Lorenzi, and Soichi 芳芽
