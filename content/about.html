Robin Tetlow-Shooter is a multidisciplinary artist working with audio-visual systems. Rooted in ambient and experimental sound, his practice explores post-human speculative physical and virtual design, investigating entropy and agency across living and non-living systems. As a sound artist and musician practising under the name Azrel, Robin constructs immersive, deconstructed sound environments that meditate on texture, stillness, and transformation.
